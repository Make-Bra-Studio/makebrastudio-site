AppQuota privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

AppQuota is an Android app by Make Bra Studio that limits the time you spend in the apps you choose. This policy explains what AppQuota reads on your phone, what it keeps and for how long, what it does not do, and what happens to an email you send us.

In short

  • Everything AppQuota reads and keeps stays on your phone.
  • AppQuota has no internet access: it does not ask Android for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. AppQuota sends no data to us, Make Bra Studio, or to anyone else.
  • The only personal data we ever receive is an email you choose to send us, and we use it only to answer you.

What AppQuota reads, and why

Usage access. With your permission, AppQuota reads Android's app usage records: which app was in front, and when. It uses them to count the time you spend in the apps you track, and it keeps nothing about the apps you don't track.

Accessibility service. When you turn it on, AppQuota's Accessibility service sees which app is in front and which windows are on screen: the app each window belongs to, the type of window, and whether it is a picture-in-picture video. AppQuota uses this only to time the apps you track and to apply the limits you set:

  • it shows its delay or block screen over a tracked app;
  • it returns to the home screen when a blocked app stays open beside its block screen (split screen, floating windows);
  • it closes a blocked app's picture-in-picture video, or brings the app back to full screen under the block screen, and pauses its playback;
  • it shows short notices over other apps, such as the minutes you have left.

The service does not read the text on your screen, what you type, your passwords or your notifications.

Your apps. AppQuota lists the apps on your phone that have an icon in the launcher, with their names and icons, so you can choose which ones to track. It also checks which app is your home screen, to tell it apart from other apps. Of this list, AppQuota keeps only the apps you choose to track.

What AppQuota keeps on your phone

  • The time you spent in each app you track: when each session started and ended. It drives your limits and the Stats screens.
  • The limits and rules you set for each tracked app, such as time caps, refills, delays and quiet hours.
  • Your Free Pass settings and when you used it.
  • The app's own settings, such as your daily reset time and how far you got through setup.

How long AppQuota keeps it

  • AppQuota keeps your history for as long as it is installed, so the Stats screens can show past weeks. It does not delete old entries on its own.
  • When you stop tracking an app, AppQuota stops recording it. The time already recorded stays until you clear AppQuota's storage.
  • Clearing AppQuota's storage or uninstalling it deletes everything it kept (see "Your choices and rights").

How it is protected

  • AppQuota keeps its data in its private app storage. Android's app sandbox stops other apps from reading it.
  • On phones that encrypt their storage, as most phones since Android 10 do, the data is encrypted with the rest of the phone's storage.
  • Since the data never leaves your phone through AppQuota, it is never exposed in transit or on a server.

What AppQuota does not do

  • It does not send your data off your phone, to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not share or sell data.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code, so no third party can collect data through it. The only libraries in it are Google's AndroidX WorkManager, which runs its background checks, and Material Components, which draws its screens. Neither sends data from AppQuota.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites, so it does nothing different when your browser or phone sends a "Do Not Track" signal.
  • It does not build a profile of you. It blocks an app only by the rules you set yourself.

Permissions

Google Play lists the permissions AppQuota asks for. This is what each one is for:

  • Usage access: to count the time you spend in the apps you track (see above). You grant it in your phone's settings, and you can take it back there at any time.
  • Accessibility: for the Accessibility service described above. You turn it on in your phone's Accessibility settings, after AppQuota shows you what it uses it for.
  • Run at startup: so AppQuota's background check starts again after your phone restarts.
  • Prevent phone from sleeping: so a background check can finish once it has started.
  • View network connections: WorkManager, the library that runs those checks, asks for this because it can wait for a network before a task. AppQuota's checks never wait for one, and AppQuota still has no internet access.

Backup and moving to a new phone

AppQuota is left out of Android's backup to your Google account. When you set up a new phone by copying from your old one, Android may copy AppQuota's data straight to the new phone, so your limits and history come with you. You start that copy yourself, it runs between your own two phones, and the data never reaches us.

Google Play

When you install AppQuota from Google Play, Google handles the download and your Google account under Google's privacy policy. Google Play shows us statistics about AppQuota, such as how many people installed it, and crash reports from phones whose owners let Android share diagnostics with Google. These come from Google, not from AppQuota.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and to fix the problem you tell us about, never for marketing.

  • We keep a conversation for as long as we need it to help you, and delete it no later than one year after it ends. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Our email is Gmail, so Google stores these messages for us and may process them outside your country.
  • We do not pass them to anyone else unless the law requires us to.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Your choices and rights

  • You don't have to give us any personal data to use AppQuota.
  • You can turn off Usage access or AppQuota's Accessibility service at any time in your phone's settings. AppQuota then stops timing or blocking apps.
  • To delete everything AppQuota keeps, open your phone's Settings › Apps › AppQuota › Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall AppQuota.
  • Everything AppQuota keeps is on your phone and under your control, and we hold no copy of it, so there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete.
  • For any emails we hold, you can ask us to show them to you, correct them, delete them or stop using them, where the law of your country gives you that right. Write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days.
  • You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

AppQuota is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If AppQuota changes how it handles data, we will post the new policy at this address with a new effective date, and describe the change in the release notes of the app update that brings it. The policy in the app always matches the version of the app you have.
